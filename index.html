A short introduction

I’ve spent 20 years close to the work—writing code, shaping products, and supporting teams.

My path started with PHP and the early web, moved through native iOS, Laravel, Vue, and Node, then widened into the craft of helping teams work well together.

Today I bring an engineer’s eye for systems and a facilitator’s care for people. I’m at my best when the problem is fuzzy, the room is full of perspectives, and there’s a better way forward waiting to be found.
